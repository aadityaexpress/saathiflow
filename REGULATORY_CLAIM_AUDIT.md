## 2. Scope Boundaries of RBI TAT Harmonisation (Sl. No. 1 vs. Sl. No. 5)

To prevent over-generalization in the research and proposal:

1. **ATM / Micro-ATM Cash Withdrawal (Annexure Sl. No. 1):**
   - Applies specifically to: "Customer's account debited but cash not dispensed in ATMs/Micro-ATMs".
   - Mandated Action: Pro-active reversal (suo motu) by acquiring bank within **T + 5 calendar days**.
   - Compensation: **₹100 per day** of delay beyond T + 5 calendar days, credited to the account holder.
2. **AePS / Aadhaar Pay (Annexure Sl. No. 5):**
   - Scenario (a): "Account debited but transaction confirmation not received at merchant location". Acquirer initiates Credit Adjustment within **T + 5 days**; ₹100/day penalty beyond T+5 days.
   - Scenario (b): "Account debited but beneficiary account not credited" (fund transfer). Standard credit-push timelines apply.
3. **What is NOT Covered by Auto-Reversal or ₹100/day Compensation:**
   - Biometric mismatch (e.g. fingerprint not matching ISO 55) where no debit occurred.
   - Core switch declines (e.g. ISO 51 insufficient balance, ISO 91 bank inoperative) where no debit occurred.
   - Off-us terminal communication dropouts prior to account debit.
   - Non-financial balance inquiries or mini-statement timeouts.
   - Transactions where cash was actually dispensed.
